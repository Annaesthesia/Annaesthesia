01 / About

Engineering with a scientist's precision

I'm a senior frontend developer and UI design engineer with more than 20 years of experience crafting responsive, accessible and high-performing websites. My background is unusual for the field: I hold an MSc in Pharmaceutical Sciences and a BSc in Analytical Chemistry, and I have worked in quality control and research settings.

That training shapes how I build. I test assumptions, document my methods, follow standard operating procedures and care about getting the details exactly right, whether the subject is a crystalline structure under a microscope or a pixel on a screen.

I'm currently pursuing the ITF-AI Level 3 certification to bring structured AI practice into my design and development workflow.
